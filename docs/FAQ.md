# Questions

## What does Morrow do?

It watches your Bitget stock loans. If one is heading for a margin call at the next reopening, it pays some down or adds backing first.

## Can it sell my stocks?

No. It can only pay down a loan with the coin you borrowed, or add more of the token already backing it. It never sells, borrows or withdraws.

## What is a margin call?

When a loan outgrows what backs it, Bitget asks you to act, and later sells your backing. Morrow reads those levels from Bitget live.

## Why does the weekend matter?

US markets close for weekends and holidays, then reopen at a new price. A loan near its limit can cross it while you are away.

## How does Morrow know what Monday looks like?

Some tokens trade all weekend and Morrow uses that price if it passes its checks. Others pause, so it uses how that stock gapped before.

## What if a weekend price looks wrong?

It checks the last trade, the spread, the orders near the price and the distance from the last close. If any check fails, it ignores that price.

## How much control do I keep?

You set limits per action, weekend and month, and pick Ask me first or Protect automatically. Pause all stops everything.

## What is a sealed promise?

Before each closure Morrow writes its plan and publishes its fingerprint at once. After the reopen it grades itself in public.

## What does it cost?

A small monthly subscription for each protected loan. The price will be shown before any billing starts.

## Can protection fail?

Yes. Protection reduces risk. It cannot remove it. With no idle balance, or past your limits, Morrow can only alert you.
